Research

From observed patterns to ecological processes: quantitative tools for biodiversity research and wildlife conservation.

Research focus

I combine Bayesian models, spatial data and ecological knowledge to study mammal abundance, distribution and habitat use. My work connects camera traps, distance sampling and hunting records, with applications in monitoring design and wildlife management.
